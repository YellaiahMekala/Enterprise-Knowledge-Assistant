Code of Conduct
Gifts and hospitality
Employees may accept gifts with a value up to 50 USD. Larger gifts must be declined or reported to compliance.
Conflicts of interest
A possible conflict of interest must be disclosed to your manager and to compliance before you act on it.
Speaking up
Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline. The company does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Data Retention
Financial records
Financial records are kept for 7 years and then deleted.
Logs and backups
Application logs are kept for 13 months. Database backups are kept for 35 days.
Deletion requests
A customer deletion request is completed within 30 days. A legal hold overrides every retention period until it is lifted by the legal team.

Payment Service Error Codes
E-4017
Error code E-4017 means the card was declined by the issuing bank. Ask the customer to use another card.
E-5002
Error code E-5002 means the payment gateway timed out. The request is safe to retry after 30 seconds.
E-6100
Error code E-6100 means the merchant account is suspended. Contact the risk team.

Expense Policy
Submission
Submit expense reports within 30 days of the expense. Reports submitted later are paid only with director approval.
Receipts
A receipt is required for every expense above 25 USD. Missing receipts must be explained in a signed statement.
Reimbursement
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days to the bank account on file.
Approval limits
Managers approve expenses up to 500 USD. Directors approve expenses up to 5,000 USD. Anything above 5,000 USD needs the CFO.
Not reimbursable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines and fuel for personal trips are never reimbursed.

Incident Response
Severity levels
SEV1 is a customer-facing outage or data exposure. SEV2 is a degraded service with a workaround. SEV3 is a minor defect without customer impact.
First response
For a SEV1 the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes and an incident commander is named within 15 minutes.
Communication
The status page is updated every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved.
Follow-up
A blameless postmortem is published within 5 business days of a SEV1 or SEV2 incident.

Leave Policy
Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March, after which they expire.
Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required when the absence lasts more than 3 consecutive days.
Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks. Leave may be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
Bereavement leave
Employees may take 5 days of paid bereavement leave after the death of an immediate family member.

Onboarding Guide
First day
Collect your laptop and badge from the front desk. Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy who answers day-to-day questions.
First weeks
Complete the security awareness and code of conduct trainings within the first 2 weeks.
Plan
Every new hire follows a 30-60-90 day plan agreed with the manager. The probation period is 6 months.
Access requests
Request system access through a ServiceHub ticket. The target time to grant standard access is 2 business days.

Procurement
Quotes
Purchases above 10,000 USD need three written quotes from different vendors.
Vendor security
Every new vendor that handles company data passes a security review before the first purchase order.
Contracts
Contracts above 50,000 USD must be reviewed by the legal team before signature.
Purchase orders
Raise a purchase order before buying. Invoices without a purchase order are returned to the requester.

Remote and Hybrid Work
Schedule
The standard hybrid schedule is 3 days in the office and 2 days remote. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time.
Equipment
New hires receive a one-time home office stipend of 400 USD. The stipend covers a desk, chair or monitor.
Connectivity
A VPN connection is required when working from outside the office network.
Working abroad
Working from another country is limited to 20 days per calendar year and needs prior approval from HR for tax and legal reasons.

Information Security Policy
Passwords
Passwords must have at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every account that can reach company data.
Keys and secrets
Service account keys are rotated every 90 days. Secrets are never stored in source code or chat messages; use the secrets manager.
Devices
All laptops use full-disk encryption. USB storage devices are blocked by default. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the security desk within 1 hour.
Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the Report Phishing button. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders.

Travel Policy
Booking
Book flights at least 14 days before departure through the approved travel agency. Bookings made later need approval from the department head.
Flights
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer with written approval from a vice president.
Hotels
The hotel limit is 180 USD per night in tier-1 cities and 150 USD per night in all other cities. Choose hotels from the approved list.
Daily allowance
A per diem of 55 USD covers meals and local transport on travel days. Meals provided by the host are deducted from the allowance.
Rental cars
Rent a compact car unless more than two travellers share the vehicle. Decline the optional insurance because the company policy already covers damage.
